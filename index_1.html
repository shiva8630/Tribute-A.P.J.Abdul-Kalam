Abdul Kalam (1931–2015) was a distinguished scientist and the 11th President of India (2002–2007), fondly known as the "Missile Man of India" and the "People's President". Born in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu, he played a pivotal role in developing India's civilian space program and military missile capabilities, including the Agni and Prithvi missiles. As a beloved leader, he was awarded the Bharat Ratna in 1997 for his immense contributions to science and technology.

Biography


  • Birth: Born on October 15, 1931, in Rameswaram, Tamil Nadu, into a modest Tamil Muslim family
  • Struggle: To support his family, he sold newspapers at a young age.
  • Education: Graduated in Physics from Saint Joseph's College, Tiruchirappalli, and later studied Aerospace Engineering at the Madras Institute of Technology
